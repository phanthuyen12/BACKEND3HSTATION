Luot 2:
0. Level 0 - Chua biet gi, muon bat dau tu dau
1. Level 1 - Da biet co ban, dang hoc nen va doc bieu do
2. Level 2 - Da giao dich nhung chua co phuong phap on dinh
3. Level 3 - Co phuong phap, dang ren ky luat va quan tri von
4. Level 4 - Muon nang cao tu duy, toi uu he thong va tam ly giao dich

Luot 3 neu khach chon level 0-1:

Luot 3 neu khach chon level 2-3:

Luot 3 neu khach chon level 4:
Ban dang o giai doan can toi uu tu duy, tam ly va he thong giao dich nhieu hon la hoc lan rong them.
